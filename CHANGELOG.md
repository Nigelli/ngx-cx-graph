## [Unreleased]

### Added
- `<cx-graph>` component: pan and zoom, collapsible nested groups with paging and lazy loading, detail card on hover or pin, edge filtering, search, minimap and keyboard navigation.
- Built-in layered layout with nested containers, and the `CxLayoutEngine` interface for other engines.
- Theming through `--cx-*` CSS custom properties, with presets for Nebular, Angular Material and PrimeNG.
- `layout: false` on an edge type, for edges that are drawn but do not decide where nodes go.
- Sample apps for Nebular, Angular Material and PrimeNG.
